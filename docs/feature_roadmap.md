## F04 historical reporting

Moving an item reclassifies its existing entries to the new category. Historical
Dashboard actuals follow that classification, while applied monthly budget allocations
remain unchanged. See the [F04 spec](../specs/v3-004-dashboard-reporting/spec.md).
